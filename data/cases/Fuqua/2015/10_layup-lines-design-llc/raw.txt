• Three friends, Laura, Isabel, and Dom, are creating a t-shirt company, Layup Line 
Designs, LLC in Nashville, TN. They are annoyed with the lack of “cool” Nashville 
based t-shirts in the market, and have decided to take matters into their own hands. 
They have hired us to think through key considerations in starting this company.
Prompt #1:
• If asked the following background information should be given: want to stay local, 
have no experience in small business, are planning to both design and screen print 
but will source the actual t-shirt, Nashville MSA is 1.8M, 13M tourists visit/year
• Key considerations that should be included: profit analysis, competitor landscape, 
industry trends, target customer

Layup Line Designs could grow into a 
business large enough so that they could 
quit their respective day jobs. If they each 
want to earn minimally 50,000/year, how 
many t-shirts would they need to sell per 
year? Per week?

costs/unit profit);

• Unit profit = $20.00
• Weekly volume = 8000/50 = 160

===== PDF p122 =====
Layup Line Designs LLC
118
• Based on the analyses performed and insights derived thus far, what would you 
recommend to Laura, Izzy, and Dom in terms of starting up Layup Line Designs?
Recommendation 
• Could easily breakeven if do not want to quit current jobs. If want to quit normal jobs, 
volume increases dramatically. Most likely infeasible as would need to hire more 
people, would need to rent more space, would require a large portion of the 
population to buy a t-shirt.
• Strong rec will return to framework to illustrate why infeasible
