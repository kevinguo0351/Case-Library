Interviewer guidance on Exhibits
• Interviewer should steer towards this being a 
cost savings case of their back-office functions
• In order to identify focus / priority areas , the 
interviewee should look at the total spend 
across all the back-office functions and identify 
functions where the client is spending the most
• The interviewee should quickly identify that IT 
and Finance contribute to 70% of the total G&A 
OpEx
• Steer the interviewee towards IT being their 
priority function to focus on first and push the 
interviewee to tell you how they would go about 
estimating the savings potential

benchmarking (if not steer towards it) and

• Analysis –
• Show exhibit 1 when the interviewee asks for 
the spend breakdown

should quickly identify that IT and Finance 
contribute to 70% of the total G&A OpEx
