Interviewer guidance on Exhibits
• US Sales Analysis report
• If the interviewee asks about the column titles, 
tell them that they represent different steps in 
the sales process
• Drive interviewee to calculating conversion 
ratios
• Q4 Test Drives (TD) to bookings is lowest. This 
is a possible cause for a drop of sales in Q4
• Once the candidate as identified that the TD to 
Bookings numbers are off in Q4, ask them to 
brainstorm what is causing this (e.g. specific 
region underperforming, unexpected Q4 
turnover, etc.) how they could solve the problem 
(e.g. altered incentives for employees, discounts 
on overstocked models, etc.)
• Feel free to push back on the brainstorming 
ideas to help lead the candidate to the best 
choice of the alternatives they brainstorm
