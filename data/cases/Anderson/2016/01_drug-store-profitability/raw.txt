UCLA Anderson Casebook 2016

Business Problem Statement
Our client is a drug store chain, similar to CVS, They are losing profits in the last few years. Can you help us
identify the reasons and means to improve the profits?
Interviewer Guidance
The candidate should answer the following questions during the course of the case discussion:
•
Is the loss of profitability due to product mix, store mix, increasing costs or decreasing revenue? Or a 
combination of all
•
What can the company do to improve profits – focused discussion around one area of improvement from 
above list
Information Provided to Interviewee Upon Request
Store details
Stores are typical to CVS, located in several areas. They have pharmacy, health and beauty, and general 
merchandise departments.
Sales and profitability by square feet and product type
