Booz Operations, Round 1
The client is an electric battery manufacturer based in 
China and is thinking to make electric cars to sell. The 
CEO thinks that he can capture world market and be a 
majority player. 
Company is world leader in Lithium batteries and had 
great success with cell phones. Using same technology 
they have developed a bigger battery that can be used 
very cost effectively for an electric car.
The client has already bought a plant in China to make
Problem statement narrative
Guidance for interviewer and 
information provided upon request(1) 
Should they launch this car?
If yes, what strategy should they use to launch this car? 
The client has already bought a plant in China to make 
these cars. The plant cost $5M and will need additional 
$1M to retrofit for electric car production. The total 
variable cost for each electric car is $7,000. The plant 
currently has capacity to produce 1000 cars per annum
To expand capacity, it will need similar investments for 
every 1000 car capacity.
The company wants to launch the car worldwide. It has 
no other partners in any country as of now.
The company wants minimum profit of $2000 per car. 
Similar category gas car sells from $16,000 to $20,000 
across world.
