Possible summary
There is about $225 million of parking business at O’Hare airport, one example of a major 
metropolitan airport. If we are able to take advantage of our knowledge gained from urban 
parking, this large, and probably growing, market could present a real opportunity for our 
growth. We need to look carefully at the competitive landscape and the economics at each 
airport location and evaluate local competition and costs. From today’s analysis, this appears an 
opportunity to explore further.
