McKinsey & Company, Round I
Lets do some calculations.  
Right now, we make $1000 Revenue/Product, Have $160 
Million in profit, and service 2 products per customer. 
The current cost structure is 20% profit, 20% fixed, and 
60% variable.  
Given the services you have talked about we predict that 
customers will increase to 600,000 expected customers, 
3 products per customer, the revenue per product stays 
the same, the variable cost per product stays the same, 
what is the expected profit?
