(Mckinsey - Round 2) 
 
I. Case scope provided by interviewer:  
 
Your client is a ferryboat company that has been approached by a 
city major to bid on a 50 year exclusivity right to service commuters 
who wish to cross the river (see map below). So far there is only one 
bridge crossing the river and traffic during peak hours is just 
horrible. Therefore the mayor sees this bid as an easy and cheap way
