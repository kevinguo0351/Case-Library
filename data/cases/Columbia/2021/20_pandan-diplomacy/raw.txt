Case #20 : PanDan Diplomacy
• Creativity
• Brainstorming
Now that President-elect Dan has won the United States general election, our client, the Chinese government, is 
constructing their approach to influence President-elect Dan to look favorably upon them in negotiations once he takes 
the oath of office. President-elect Dan is the first independent who has won a presidential election in the modern era. He 
is a Pennsylvania native, from a small town a few hours north of Philadelphia who campaigned on strong international 
trade deals, education reform, and immigration reform. One of the most effective diplomatic approaches for China has 
been Panda Diplomacy. Panda Diplomacy is the process by which the Chinese government sends giant pandas to countries 
in which to leverage better trade deals and improve their outcomes. We have been tasked to help our client sculpt their 
initial Panda Diplomacy approach to President-elect Dan. What factors would you consider when planning this Panda 
Diplomacy approach?
Problem Statement Narrative
