NeuroNow
Our client, NeuroNow, is a mid-market biopharma company having 2 products in the US market. They have developed a 
new oral medicine to treat multiple sclerosis (MS) which has successfully passed phase 2 of clinical trials. Before deciding 
on further investments in phase 3 and commercialization, our client is looking to project revenues for the drug in 2021 and 
would like to know how soon can they breakeven on their investment from phase 3 trials onwards
Problem Statement Narrative
Difficulty: Hard
Industry: Pharmaceuticals 
Case Type: Market Entry
Written by Vishesh Jain ‘21
• Market entry
• Market sizing
• Breakeven analysis
Concepts Tested
Case #17 : NeuroNow
Case Notes
This case consists of 3 main components: 
1) Setting up market sizing calculations and identifying relevant haircuts to be considered in the patient population
2) Recommending ways to estimate market share
3) Identifying risks and benefits of going ahead with further investment on phase 3 trials

What are some other risks that our client could face?
NeuroNow
Question #4
Question
•
Clinical trial failure: Profitability calculations need to be adjusted by the probability of success in the trial
•
Competitor response: A fast-growing oral MS drug market would attract existing injectable competitor to develop 
new oral products and new players to entire this market; Our position can be swayed if new competition arises which 
provides one or many of better clinical utility, economic value or ease to various relevant stakeholders
•
Regulatory risks: Industry is very exposed to such changes
•
Supply: Manufacturing and/or distribution related issues either internally or with partners
