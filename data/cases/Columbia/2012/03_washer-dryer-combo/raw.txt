experienced sluggish growth over the past decade. You have risen through the ranks, starting in corporate

put clothes in and set it to both wash and dry). You predict the board will ask you what you want to do with 
this potential new product, and you have 15 minutes in traffic to figure out what to do.

Question #2:  
Question #2 Solution:  
Evaluate the economics of the combined washer /dryer product. 
The combined product is less profitable than the traditional 2-unit product.  Therefore, your company will 
need to capture additional market share to boost total profits by offering this product.  A breakeven analysis 
can be used to evaluate the feasibility of these gains: 
 
Assume your urban consumers will prefer the new product. 
 
Loss = $50/unit * 6.4M Units * 25% market share = $80M 
Breakeven in number of units = $80M / $150 = 0.53M Units 
Breakeven in required market share capture = 6.4M Units per year / 0.53M Units = ~12% 
Current share = 25%; 12% share represents ~50% growth in share

Questions #3 & 4:  
Questions #3 & 4 Solutions:  
#3 - Based on the work you have done, what actions do you suggest your company take in relations to the 
new combined product? 
 
#4 - The R&D VP has told you now that they have sunk $20 million so far into the project. Unfortunately, 
they aren’t sure how much more money will need to be allotted to this project to get it to market. Not only 
that, but the company has seen many CEOs come and go over the past few years. This decision will be 
one of your first and most impactful decisions at the helm of the company. Now what would your response 
be? 
The idea here is to ask for conclusions based on available information then create doubt in the 
interviewee’s mind and force them to defend that conclusion. This is a classic case of interviewing with a 
Partner who doesn’t care as much about the analytics but more about strategic rationale and sticking to 
your guns.   One answer might be : “I would reassure the Board that I plan on being CEO for the long 
haul. I would remain committed to the product and use this product as a way to signal a new era for the 
firm”.
