Cheapo Coffee 
Case Commentary (Notes to the interviewer) 
Problem Statement Narrative 
None. 
Data Set (To be provided upon request) 
Cheapo Coffee roasts and packages coffee that it sells through coffee shops and grocery stores. Cheapo 
sells its product to these retailers and provides the labor to stock the shelves at store locations. The 
company has seen sales growth stagnate and profits decline over the last several years. What is causing 
the decline and how can Cheapo Coffee raise its profits? 
Go through the interviewee’s initial framework 
with them and probe on one area for further 
brainstorming. 
 
After the initial framework, the interview will be 
based mostly on the exhibits.

Question #1:  
Question #1 Solution:  
(Hand Exhibits 1, 2, and 3 to the interviewee.) 
1)
Based on these Exhibits, what hypothesis can you form about the cause of Cheapo’s profit margin 
declines? 
2)
How would this product shift impact the Company’s cost structure? 
3)
What information would you need to confirm this hypothesis? 
Sales growth is slowing, but not declining so this must be a cost issue. 
1)
Exhibit 3 shows that the product mix has shifted toward Private Label as a share of total revenue. 
Because these products are the cheapest by retail price, this means that the unit volume of private 
label has increased dramatically. The shift toward private label caused costs to increase and 
profitability to decline. 
2)
An increase in the amount of volume handled would cause an increase in every type of variable cost 
incurred by the company. Major cost increases would come from the labor hours required to stock 
additional product (where before staff stocked a lower number of branded packages for the same level 
of sales). Transportation costs and roasting costs would also increase. 
3)
I would need a cost break-down for each product type.

Cheapo makes $3.00 on branded packages ($15*20%), $1.80 on Closeouts, and only $0.80 on

Cheapo Coffee 
 
o Lower the labor and transportation cost of providing Private Label coffee to retailers. Shelf Private 
   Label products in a bargain bin instead of on shelves to reduce stocking time. 
o Attempt to shift sales back toward product lines with a higher absolute profit level. Place private 
   label product in a lower traffic area of the store. Raise prices on Private Label to reflect costs. 
Note: Give the interviewee a chance to get all of these ideas (and more) by asking “What else?” 
Recommendation 
 
o Retailer incentives may not match Cheapo’s incentives. Retailers could use the Private Label 
   coffee as a marketing tool to show their good value or to drive traffic and push back against plans 
   to reduce volume or raise prices. 
 
Risks 
 
o Benchmark costs for Private Label products produced by competitors. 
 
o Approach retailers and grocery stores to negotiate either a price increase or a means of lowering 
   costs (bargain bin display, stocking responsibility completed by retailer instead of Cheapo, etc.).
