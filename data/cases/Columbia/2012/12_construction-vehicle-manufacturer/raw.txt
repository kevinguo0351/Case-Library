Conclusion 
 
 
Construction Vehicle Manufacturer 
 
The candidate can suggest that the firm look to keep its presence in the low growth, high market  
share countries playing in the market of replacement trucks perhaps. 
 
The firm could also decide to pursue the high growth, low market share countries. They could do this by using 
 the extra capacity that they will free up by exiting the low growth, low market share countries. They can exit by  
raising prices for these countries, since they wont care if they switch to competitors. 
 
Recommendation 
o Replacement market may not be as big of an opportunity as expected 
 
o Market dynamics could change 
 
o Lower price might not result in increased capacity 
Risks 
 
o Verify assumptions about the replacement market, market growth rates and price elasticity  
 
o Explore other ways to shift the dynamics (can we buy additional plants, change our operations?) 
 
o Verify that capacity can be shifted dynamically between countries (maybe there are regulations?)
