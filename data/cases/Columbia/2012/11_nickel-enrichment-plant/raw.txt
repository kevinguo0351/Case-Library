Case: Nickel Enrichment Plant 
Your client is Nickel Inc., a company in the nickel enrichment industry in Brazil. Its business consists of: 
•
purchasing brute nickel and nickel additives from producers; 
•
transporting them to Nickel Inc.’s plant in Southern Brazil; 
•
enriching the brute nickel at the plant; and 
•
delivering the enriched nickel to factories in Southeastern Brazil 
Nickel Inc. is currently the #3 nickel enrichment company in Brazil, producing a total of 10 tons of 
enriched nickel per month. Due to increasing demand, Nickel Inc. wants to increase its production to 20 
tons per month. 
Candidate should identify that this is a cost 
case. No revenue analysis is needed 
Case is Math-heavy. It also tests the candidate’s 
creativity and ability to identify and ask for 
relevant information 
Will be provided in later portions of the case.

Nickel Inc. has determined that the only two alternatives to increase production are expanding its 
existing plant or building a new plant in Southeastern Brazil. What factors would you consider in 
deciding between these two options? 
Some factors candidates should mention: 
•
Costs (variable and fixed) are a major factor, as well as the initial investment required in each case 
•
New plant would be closer to factories, potentially decreasing transportation costs 
•
Cost of nickel and additives may vary between two locations (different transportation costs, 
producers, etc) 
•
There might be economies of scale in having a plant producing 20 tons, instead of two plants 
producing 10 tons 
•
There might be additional costs to running two plants instead of one
