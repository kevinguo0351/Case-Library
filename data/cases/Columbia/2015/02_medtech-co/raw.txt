MedTech Co.
Question #1
[Give interviewee Exhibit 1] Which business units would you invest in given the info in Exhibit 1?
Question #1
•
Interviewee should take into account current size, growth and relative market share.
•
Interviewee should ask for profit information (if she/he does not, interviewer should lead her/him).
•
After interviewee asks for profit information, interviewer should give Exhibit 2.
•
Interviewee should analyze information in the Exhibits while explaining to interviewer, formulating a 
recommendation based on the data in the Exhibit.
•
Solution for Exhibit 2:
•
Knee and hip both enjoy relatively high market share, growth and profit margins and are therefore good 
for further investments. Trauma may also be taken into account due to its high level of growth.

===== PDF p16 =====
MedTech Co.
Conclusion
• The Knee and Hip units both enjoy the most 
market share, positive growth and a high profit 
margin.
Rationale
• MedTech should invest in the Knee and/or Hip 
units. 
Recommendation
• Begin evaluation of investment opportunities in 
Knee / Hip (new products etc.)
Next Steps
• Since Knee and Hip already have relatively large 
market share, the market in these departments 
may be reaching potential. MedTech should also 
consider investing in Trauma which is enjoying 
very high growth and has a relatively high profit 
margin.
• Competitor attack – competitors could follow 
MedTech and also invest in knee or hip.
