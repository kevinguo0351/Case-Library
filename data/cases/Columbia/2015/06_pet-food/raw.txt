Question #1
How many dogs are there in the US?
Question #1
Provide exhibit 2. 
Calculation:
No of US households 100M * 40% (exhibit 1) = 40M households with dogs
No of households with 1 dog = 40M *  58% = 23.2M  23.2M dogs
No of households with 2-3 dogs = 40M * 36% = 14.4M average 2.5 dogs: 14.4M * 2.5 = 36M dogs
No of households with 4+ dogs = 40M *  7% = 2.8M okay to use 4 dogs: 2.8M * 4 = 11.2M dogs
Total dogs: 70.4M
(FYI – this number is approximately correct, according to the American Society for the Prevention of Cruelty 
to Animals)

Question #2
What is the market for dog food in the US?
Question #2
Provide exhibits 3 and 4; rounding the numbers is allowed and encouraged!
Average ounces/pounds of dog food per day (from exhibit 3):
(4+8+14+17+32)/5 = 75/5 = 15 ounces per day on average
* 16 ounces in 1 pound (see bottom of exhibit), okay to round to 1 pound per day
1 pound * 360 days a year = 360 pounds a year
Average price of dog food per pound (exhibit 4):
Ol’ Roy: 18.97 / 40  ~ 20/40 = $0.5 dollars per pound
Kibbles ‘n Bits: 13.49 / 17.6 ~14/17.5 =  $0.8 dollars per pound
Eukanuba: 39.99 / 40 ~ 40/40 = $1 per pound
Blue Buffalo: 49.99 / 33 ~ 30/50 = $0.6 per pound
(0.5+0.8+1+0.6)/4 = 2.9/4 = ~ $0.75 per pound on average
360 pounds * $0.75 = $270 per dog per year
$270 * 70M dogs = $1.89B

Question #3
What is the yearly demand for dog food at Walmart and Safeway?
Question #2
Provide exhibit 4; Additional assumptions to be given upon interviewee questions:
•
Safeway has ~10M yearly customers; Walmart has ~100M customers
•
Can assume that 40% of Safeway / Walmart customers have dogs (Exhibit 1 data)
•
10% of customers who have dogs buy their dog food at Safeway / Walmart 
Safeway:
Average consumption is 360 pounds per year (question 1), Safeway brand has 17.6 pounds a bag at $13.5. 
Interviewee can and should round to 20lbs and $15
360 / ~20 = 18 no of bags each customer buys a year
18 * $15 = $270 per year spent on dog food 
4M Safeway customers have dogs, 400K buy their dog food at Safeway: 400K*18 = 7.2M bags sold; 
7.2M * $270 = $1.9B in revenue from dog food
Walmart:
Walmart brand has 40lbs a bag at $18.97 (can round to $20)
360 / 40 = 9 no of bags each customer buys a year (notice – exactly half of Safeway)
9 * $20 = $180 per year spent on dog food
40M Walmart customers have dogs, 4M buy their dogfood at Walmart: 4M*9 = 36M bags sold; 
36M * $180 = $6.4B in revenue from dog food
