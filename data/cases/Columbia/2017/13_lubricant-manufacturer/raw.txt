Question #2
To produce Type 3 lubricant for the automotive industry, the client would need to invest in a new production 
facility:
•
Fixed cost of the new production facility: $10M
•
Variable costs: $10 / lb
•
Market price of the lubricant: $50 / lb
•
Average purchase by barrel: 1 barrel = 5 gallons
•
4 lbs = 1 gallon
How many barrels would the client need to sell to break even within 2 years?
Question #2
Unit profit: $50 - $10 = $40/lb
Fixed cost / Unit profit = $10M / $40 = 250,000 lbs
Converting from lbs to gallons: 250,000 lbs / 4 = 62,500 gallons
Converting from gallons to barrels: 62,500 gallons / 5 = 12,500 barrels
Per year: 12,500 barrels / 2 = 6,250 barrels per year to break even
Candidate should comment on this number – is that a reasonable number of barrels to sell? (no data given, 
so this would be a guesstimate)
