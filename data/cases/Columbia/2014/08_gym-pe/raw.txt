•
The difficulty of this case depends on how it is given by the interviewer. If data is easily provided, then the breakeven 
and the case are fairly straightforward.  
•
However, in the actual Bain interview, the interviewer did 3 things to make this case much more challenging:  
•
Asked the candidate to draw out the framework on a whiteboard without any time to prep 
•
Asked the candidate to do all the math without paper (rounding was acceptable) 
•
Did not provide any additional information until prompted
