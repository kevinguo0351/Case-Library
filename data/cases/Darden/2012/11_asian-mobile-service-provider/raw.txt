CASE 11:  
ASIAN MOBILE SERVICE PROVIDER 
Case Question: 
Your client is a mobile service provider in an Asian country.  They were targeting on the 
high and medium segments in the country.  Recently they acquired another service 
provider which targeting lower segment of the market.  The two companies have two 
different brands before, and they kept both brand after the merge.  To the CEO’s 
surprise, the overall pre-tax profit has been lower than the combined pre-tax profit of 
the two companies when they were not merged. 
You are hired to figure out why and how can we improve that. 
Firm Style 
Interview Round 
Bain 
2
