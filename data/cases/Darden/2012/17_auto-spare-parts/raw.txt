AUTO SPARE PARTS 
Calculations 
Size of market (note repairs include routine maintenance and 
ignores repairs due to accidents) 
• 100 MM HHs in the US 
• Average auto ownership = 2 
• Therefore – 200 MM cars in the US 
• Cars need no repair for newer cars, lots for older cars.  
• So average trip to garages = 5 per year (4 maintenance + 1 
repair) 
• Therefore – total trips to garages = 1B (200 MM cars * 5) 
• Car repairs/maintenance can go from $30 - $3000, so average 
repair (assume = $300, 50% for parts, 50% for labor),  
• Therefore, avg parts ordered per visit = $150 (ie 300/2) 
• Size of market =  
• Number of visits * $ parts ordered per visit 
• = 1 B trips * $150 parts ordered per visit 
• = $ 150 B USD annually
