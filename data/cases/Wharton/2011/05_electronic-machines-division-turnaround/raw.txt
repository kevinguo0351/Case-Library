•  The electronic machine is a $1B industry, stable (not growing, not shrinking)

consumables. 
About the products: 
•  There are 2 types of machines: old technology  (it has been in the market for 40 years) and new 
technology  (10 years) 
•  90% of the market is old technology. Despite the benefits of the new technology, customers are 
not willing to pay the price 
•  Prices: old technology sold at $1,800 and new technology sold at $2,800 
About the company: 
•  They entered the market 10 years ago, by inventing the new technology.  Hence, 60% of their 
revenues comes from the new technology machines sales. 
•  Other business units are more profitable

Case 5: Electronic Machines Division Turnaround 
Conclusions 
50 
What would it be your overall recommendation to the client? 
To move the production from the U.S. to Malaysia, to reduce costs and improve EBITA to 20%. 
Negotiate with distributors, offer regional exclusivities. And once the operation is stable (quality, distribution, etc), the company can 
decrease the price (or run promotions) to drive demand from the old technology to the new technology in the US, as the current obstacle 
on replacing old technology is primarily due to pricing. 
At the new price, they could sell the new machines in Asia,  under a different  brand, and generate extra revenues. 
Risks: Competitors can lower price as well.
