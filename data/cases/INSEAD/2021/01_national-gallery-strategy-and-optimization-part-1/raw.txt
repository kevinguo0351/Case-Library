Step 2: Calculate revenue and costs under each model
Current model, with 4 exhibitions per year 
 Revenue from ticket sales: 1.64m visitors x $10 = $16.4m 
 Costs: $3m x 4 = $12m
 Net profit = $16.4m - $12m = $4.4 m
New model of reduce exhibition number to 3
 Revenue from ticket sales: 1.5m visitors x $10 = $15m 
 Costs: $3m x 3 = $9m
 Net profit: $15m - $9m = 6m 
Recommendation: Reducing the number of special exhibitions from 4 to 3 per year can drive up profitability, as costs are declining at faster rates than revenue.
