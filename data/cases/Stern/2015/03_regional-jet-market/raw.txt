Case Prompt 
While the market for long-haul jet manufacturers is a duopoly between Boeing and Airbus, the market for regional 
jet manufacturers is currently a duopoly between Bombardier of Canada and Embraer of Brazil. However, the market 
appears to be in for a period of transition due to at least two new entrants, Competitor “R” of Russia and Competitor 
“C” of China.  
 
What do you think will happen to the international market for regional jet manufacturers in the next five years?

Question #5 
What can you interpret from the 
current and future cost per plane 
of the Chinese and Russian 
competitors? 
Notes to Interviewer 
• Currently, the new entrants 
cannot compete on a cost per 
plane basis due to a lock of 
demand necessary to 
sufficiently amortize fixed 
costs 
• It may be possible that their 
governments are providing 
subsidies and tax breaks that 
are not being taken into 
account 
• By 2017, their cost per plane is 
much more in line with the 
current market conditions

Question #7 
What do you see being the drivers of 
demand for future growth in the airline 
manufacturing industry? 
 
Notes to Interviewer 
New Technology: 
• Fuel efficiency 
• Range 
• Safety 
New Regulations: 
• Existing planes are retired quicker 
• Environmental regulations 
• Subsidies to make any part of the air travel value 
chain cheaper 
• Subsidies for airplane manufacturing 
New Demand: 
• Customers from emerging countries having 
more disposable income 
• Increase in the price of substitutes such as trains 
• General economic growth causing more 
consumption from developed countries
