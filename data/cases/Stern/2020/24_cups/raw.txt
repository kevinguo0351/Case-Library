Case Prompt: 
Your client is Proctor & Gamble, which owns Tampax and Always, leading menstrual product brands in the US. 
With the rise in sustainability focused products, P&G’s in-house R&D team has developed a hypoallergenic and 
eco-friendly high-grade silicone material for use in upcoming products launches. They are interested in using the 
material to make menstrual cups to add to their line of consumer care products. Should they sell the silicone 
menstrual cups?

Question 3: Case Math 
Given the information in Exhibit B and Exhibit C, how should P&G price the menstrual cup in order to avoid 
negative cannibalization of current menstrual care product sales?
Math Solution: 
Math Information: 
Breakeven Unit Price = (Cannibalized Revenue + R&D Costs -
Estimated Complimentary Revenue)/Captured User Market
•
Cannibalized Revenue = $675M
•
R&D Costs = $12M
•
Estimated Complementary Revenue = $637.5
○
$675M + $12M - $637.5M = $49.5M
○
Breakeven Unit Price = $49.5M/1.65M = $30/cup
•
Interviewee should approximate 
break-even price per unit 
between $25-30
