Case 4: Silverado 
Your client, Silverado, is a manufacturer of silverware that is sold to households and restaurants. The company has been in the industry for 
over 80 years and has a very strong brand name. Silverado has experienced declining revenue growth for the past 5 years. Silverado’s 
CEO would like to understand why this is the case and how the company can restore its previous high revenue growth rates. 
This is a case that the candidate will need to lead but with the use of some directive questioning as well. The goal is to determine the cause of 
declining growth and provide suggestions to restore growth. Guide the candidate to begin with exploration of market size and Silverado’s 
relative market share to competitors. After clarifying the question, the candidate should understand that the key to profitability is not associated 
with reducing costs. 
 
A strong candidate will quickly interpret the Revenue and Growth charts and list hypotheses for declining revenue growth without being prompted. 
The candidate should also be able to generate additional hypotheses regarding declining revenue if asked. A superior candidate will use 
estimated % growth rates for luxury vs. generic silverware to support their claim that growth in generic silverware has slowed. The candidate 
should also benchmark the growth associated with additional revenue from a price change in the luxury market with historical revenue growth 
rates.  At the conclusion, the candidate should brainstorm other creative ideas to grow revenue such as introducing a middle tier product line or 
via an acquisition.

Question #2 
Sample Analysis 
Please interpret the market and revenue/cost charts. 
• Growth in generic segment has slowed down 
• Gross margin in luxury segment is more than twice the gross margin in generic segment 
• Gross margin = (revenue – cogs)/revenue 
• Luxury revenue = 150 * 180,000 = $27M 
• Generic revenue = 25 * 1,000,000 = $25M 
• Luxury gross margin = ($27M – 13.77M) / (27M) = 49% or ~50% 
• Generic gross margin = ($25M – 19.75M) / (25M) = 21% or ~20%
