Case 13: Post-Patent Pharma Problem 
Your client is a manufacturer of over-the-counter drugs. They have two products: ADR and ZYK. Lately, trends in the industry and the 
company are uninspiring - sales are flat and costs are creeping upward.  How can the company do better? 
In this case the candidate will be required to explore market share, market size, revenue, gross margin, and various costs for each product and 
then calculate margin and net profit for each of the two products. A superior candidate will have the various categories of costs in the 
pharmaceutical industry included in his or her framework. When discussing the additional revenue necessary to make ADR profitable, the 
candidate should note that this is a significant increase and would be difficult to achieve without a targeted growth strategy.  The candidate 
should understand that the case is looking to compare two different strategies for making ADR profitable, increasing revenue and cutting costs 
and based on the numbers, cutting costs appears to be more feasible. Strategies on how to both increase revenues and cut costs should be 
hypothesis driven and the final recommendation should highlight that if there is no feasible plan to bring ADR to profitability, the drug should be 
shut down. A superior candidate should also note the next steps involved with the suggested strategy for ADR.
