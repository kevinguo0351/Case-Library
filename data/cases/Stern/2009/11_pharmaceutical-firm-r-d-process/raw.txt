11. Pharmaceutical Firm - R&D process _________________  
Type of Case: Operation  
Difficulty: Moderate 
 
Opening:  
Satellite research labs send compounds to centralized analysis center. Analysis center draws 
blood from animals, analyzes the results, and then sends results back to research lab – iterative 
process for each compound (5 to 15 reviews per compound). 15 year patent begins after the 
1st review/iteration if compound passes initial tests during 1st review/iteration. There tends to be 
a long queue of compounds waiting to be analyzed  
Background:  
Questions from interviewer: Objective is to reduce the cycle time.What are ways to reduce the 
cycle time and what are the benefits to the company of doing so?  
Provide the following information if requested by interviewee.  
• The research center recently hired more people (can also think about increasing capacity 
at the analysis center), but cycle time did not change.  
• The scientists heard that more people were hired, so they sent more compounds.  
• Number of compounds that passed the 1st review did not increase.  
• Scientists starting sending low quality compounds (likelihood of going to market is very 
low) and queue remained long.  
• Scientists can usually predict the likelihood of each compound passing the 1st review.  
• There was no prioritization of the queue – it was first come, first serve.  
Interviewer should come up with the idea of Prioritizing Compounds  
Prioritize compounds: by using previous results or scientist’s confidence of passing review a 
compounds with highest likelihood of going to market should jump the queue.  
This discouraged scientists from sending low quality compounds.  
<method of prioritization is less important than understanding the need to prioritize>  
<Once interviewee determines that prioritization is needed, interviewer should provide the 
following information, and ask interviewee to quantify cycle time decrease>  
Currently:  
30% of compounds require 15 cycles  
70% of compounds require 5 cycles

After the prioritization:  
Cycle time was 3.5 weeks per iteration, went down to 2 weeks.  How many weeks on average 
this save per compound?  
Recommendation <Interviewee should translate cycle time decrease into business 
implications>  
Saved 12 weeks for an average compound.  If a drug ever goes to market, 12 weeks of revenue is 
captured.  The key issue is that revenue for a drug has a short life, based on the 15 year patent.  
Adding 12 weeks of revenue is a significant increase to the companies’ revenue stream.
