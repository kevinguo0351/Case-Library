16. Crunch CEO Presentation                 _________________  
Type of Case: Marketing 
Difficulty: High 
 
 
You have 20 minutes to prepare a presentation to give to a CEO of a media company. The client 
has the No 1 electronic components catalog. This catalog is distributed at no cost to product 
designers. Revenues come from ads in the catalog. The CEO has noticed that revenues have 
declined 60% year over year. The CEO feels that because the quality of the product, they are a 
content service provider. He is interested in converting the catalog into an online publication. He 
has come to you to determine if this business makes sense and how he should market it. 
 
Additional Information acquired through questions. 
The company has editorial freedom, so they aren't tied to the products they advertise. 
The content is used by designers to determine specifications of components that may be used in 
product designs. This information is well-respected in the industry and typically seen as valuable 
to developers. 
The company would not get as much ad revenue from an online product, because advertisers 
would not pay as much for online ads. 
If they went online, they would have to change to a subscription or pay model, meaning 
developers would have to pay to access the information. Would the customers be interested in 
paying? 
Charge the developers for access to specification information. 
We are first to market. 
Because it's online, the info will have to be updated more frequently. The inventory needs of 
developers can be relayed to manufacturers, which may be valuable.

Two competitors in online space: 
 
Competitor A: had been in business for 2 years with 75% market share. Electronics distributor. 
Reliability of their information was questionable. They had a lot of data and access to OEMs, but 
because they are an electronics distributor they had conflicts of interests and there were questions 
about their ability to objectively provide product information and recommendations. 
 
Competitor B: in the business for 1 year with 25% market share. The same data as we have, but 
at a higher price point than us. They also lacked functionality of our client. Our client had more 
robust search capabilities.

RECOMMENDATION:  
 
Based on the core competency not being violated by changing their business model, it's just 
being converted and it's not drastically different from their core competency. Providing valuable 
information to their customers. They are differentiated enough to enter the space quickly and 
gain market share. They should enter the market.  
 
The consulting partner made the recommendation, and the company said they would move 
forward if they could recover a $6M initial investment within 3 years.  
 
What are things that they have to consider. Are they going to make their money back?  
 
What was their estimated market share? 15% for each year.  
Estimated to have 10% operating margin.  
 
Result: 18M first year, 24M second year, and 36M third year. 
Op Margin: 7.8M over three years, which is greater than the $6M investment, so they should 
move forward with the project.
