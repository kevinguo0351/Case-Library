10. Pharma                                                  _________________  
Type of Case: Supply Chain/ Operations  
Difficulty: Moderate 
 
Client is a pharma product mfg for over the counter drugs. They have two products: big line and 
small line. Trends in industry and company are uninspiring. Flat growth and costs are creeping 
upward.  
(Interviewee) So I assume that means we’re seeing a decline in profitability  
(Interviewer) – yes, that’s a good assumption.  
(interviewee) – I will do a profit tree)  
Market Customer  
-customer preferences  
-share of the market  
-anything in the pipeline  
-competitors in the OTC market  
 
Asked about products themselves. What market size is and share for company.  
Product A – $1.2 B  
Product B - $700 M  
Our size is one third of product A, and $150 of product B  
Interviewee may use five forces to understand pressures  
Competitors and their sizes  
Market growing  
How has our share been changing  
 
Answers were all things were constant. Share flat. Market flat.  
Interviewer should then analyze costs.  
Suggested that fixed costs are R&D, production, labor, Mfg. Variable costs could be raw 
materials and distribution

Interviewer provides following info:   
Product A has gross margin of 40%, fixed costs of $180 M  
Product B has gross margin of 60%, fixed costs of $70 M  
Using this, calculated total margins by multiplying percentages by total revenue. We see that 
Product A is netting -$20 M, and Product B is netting $20 M. (Drew table)  
Either try increasing revenues or decreasing costs.  
Sell more, increase prices, diversify product line, synergies between two products, increase 
geographic scope, acquire competitor, etc.  
Answers were that no options were good, but the final recommendation is to shut down product 
A. This answer was the last resort option and could only be reached by exhausting all possible 
avenues.  
How much additional revenue would you need to make Product A profitable.  Calculated that 
and concluded that an extra $50 M in revenue would break even.
