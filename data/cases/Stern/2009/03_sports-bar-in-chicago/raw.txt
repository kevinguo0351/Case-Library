3. Sports Bar in Chicago                                             ________  
Type of Case: New Business/ Profitability  
Difficulty: Low  
   
Opening  
Your client is an entrepreneur and looking to invest in new bar.  He needs to determine how 
profitable the company will be, and convince his primary investor, his father, that it will be a 
viable business.  What are the areas you would consider/ investigate?  
   
Background (Provide the following information if requested by interviewee.)  
Interviewer should take minute to structure:  
Investment standpoint  
a. Is there an expected return? What return do you think you can make?  
b. Is father going to be active in management? No  
Customers/Marketing  
a. What type of bar is it going to be? Sports Bar  
b. Who is my customer base? Upscale men, aged 28-40  
c. Will it be open at night, day, food/no food?  Hours below, will have food service.  
d. What is the product mix? (Food and drink)  
Location: Chicago (interviewer leads: How would you find a good place in Chicago? What 
resources would you use?)  
a. Proximity of competitors  
b. Talk to real estate agents  
i. Figure out what type of properties curious  
c. Where does customer segment live  
d. Segment by district  
    Areas of Discussion  
[Interviewer re-directs to framework, asking what else might interviewee consider to 
determine viability of bar]  
Revenues? (Interviewer asks for sources of revenue)  
a. Food? Interviewer asks how would we project how much we might earn?  
i. Look at capacity  
ii. Look at benchmarking of other food/bar restaurants  
iii. How much does ave food customer spend? $15/ea  
iv. 10 food customers/hour (case use peak/non peak hours for extra difficulty)  
b. Drinks?  
i. How much does average customer spend? $20/ea  
ii. 4 drinks customers/hour  
c. Sun-Wed competitor numbers  
i. b/ween 12pm-8pm  
1. 10 food customers/hr  
2. 4 drink customers/ hr
