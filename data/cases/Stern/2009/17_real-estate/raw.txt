17. Real Estate                                            _________________  
Type of Case: Real Estate Investment  
Difficulty: High 
East Asian development company. Sold off US division in the property services division, which 
used to make $100M / year in pretax earnings (EBIT). Looking to replace these lost profits by 
looking at real estate investment management. CEO wants to know if you can replace these 
profits and if it’s a good fit for the business. Do you know what the real estate investment 
management business is?  
(Quick explanation) Managers create fund. Investors invest in it. Investments are real estate 
assets. The fund makes money on 2/20. 2% of assets are paid to you every year and then 20% of 
excess returns are paid to management company. This is only applicable to “excess returns.” 
They benchmark against anything, probably S&P or other applicable industry index, and the 
company gets anything above the industry/index average. For example, S&P is 8%, so firm gets 
20% of 1% if the fund returns 9%.  
  Four different areas from a chart:    
• Investors 
• Fund 
• Properties  
• Firm  
Need to focus on the investors because those revenues need to contribute to the 2/20 that will 
make up the $100 M in lost profits. The 2/20 must equal or exceed $100M. Need to focus on 
what talent you need to get these investors, what investors you’ll need (if they exist), how to 
structure the firm to get these later.  
Once you get into that, then you have to figure out the amount of assets necessary to make $100 
M pretax. Need to get costs, which he found were 60% of the revenue were costs. So the total 
revenue needs to be $250 M. Then need to understand what are standard returns and what are our 
expected returns. Typical returns are 10% and our expected returns are 20%. So excess returns 
are 10%.  
2% (AUM) + 20% (AUM x 10%) = $250. AUM = $6.25 B  
Soft portion of case: what talent needed, how to handle international barrier, other than money 
what to offer.
