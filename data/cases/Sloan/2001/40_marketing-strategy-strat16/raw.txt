Outline of Your Answer - Important questions asked, additional information divulged, 
assumptions, and logic:  [Walk us through how you arrived at your answer in detail.] 
 
A problem of new product introduction is best approached with the 4 P’s tool.  
To start with, however, it is important to understand what market you will be working in and what 
therefore your marketing strategy should be.  
The market:  
− who are your customers and what is their purchasing pattern; 
− how big is the potential market; 
− who are your competitors and what are their strategies; 
− what are our strong and weak points comparing to those of competition; 
− what are our costs of manufacturing the new product; 
− what are the future market trends? 
 
Your marketing strategy should be then based on those facts:
