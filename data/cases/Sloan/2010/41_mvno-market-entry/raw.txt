41.) MVNO Market Entry (BeG, Round 2) 
Stem: In 2003, a client asked us to evaluate whether or not they should enter the US wireless 
market. Our client was an international wireless carrier that successfully operates in Asia and 
was looking for international growth opportunities. They came to us to understand 1) whether or 
not they should enter and 2) what key challenges they might face upon entry_ 
Our client intends to enter the market using an arrangement known as an MVNO (Mobile Virtual 
Network Operator), in which they will lease network capacity from an existing wireless network 
operator in the US, referred to as an MNO (mobile network operator) or Host Operator.

The company will need to invest heavily in marketing and advertising campaigns to

Need to reward loyal customers and encourage refelTals

Will need to be powerful enough to overcome switching costs of changing carriers,

Interviewer: The company has no existing relationships with customers and will likely have to

• 
Online: While an important, low cost channel, most customers will still want to

electronic devices sold in their channel

Non-traditional (e.g., high-end department stores, jewelry stores ): Although these
