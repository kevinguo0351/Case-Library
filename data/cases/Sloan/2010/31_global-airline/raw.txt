Key insight candidate should make on their own: The payback period is about 1 year, regardless

about $330M ($1800*365 days*500 planes). 
Recommendation: The investment in wireless service on international flights is attractive 
because the payback period is only 1 year and annual profit is about $330M. Revenue potential 
could be even greater due to the secondary effects of adding such a service - more passengers 
flying our airline, more passengers using the service, different pricing methods. There are some 
risks, including reliability of new technology and adoption by competing airlines.
